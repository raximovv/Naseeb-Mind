Reliable Ones

True to Their Word

You think before you promise, because a promise you make, you keep.

Which is why the thing that matters is usually handed to you.

Your strength

Keeping your word.

Worth watching

Taking every task on yourself means carrying more than your share. Asking for help is also a strength.

How this character is derived

This character comes from your two strongest traits: Agreeableness and Conscientiousness. You have the other three as well. These two simply scored higher.

If those two traits score very closely together, the test shows you the second character too. The result is a snapshot of today, not a fixed label.

At home At home and with the people close to you

Your strengths

  • You do it without being asked When you see a job around the house, you do not wait to be told.
  • Loyal to a promise Even a small promise you do not forget.
  • Looking after the older ones Your grandparents lean on you most of all.
  • Order in the house Where you are, nothing goes missing.
  • A quiet presence You do not speak loudly, but the moment you are not there it is noticed.

Worth keeping an eye on

  • You take the whole load Most of the housework collects on you, and you say nothing about it.
  • Guilt When you cannot manage something, you blame yourself for a long time afterwards.
  • You are unable to refuse Even worn out, you cannot bring yourself to turn it down.
  • You come last By the time everyone else’s things are done, no time is left for you.
  • You say nothing when it goes unnoticed It stings when what you did is not seen, and you do not mention it.

At school In lessons, in exams and among classmates

Your strengths

  • You are the one trusted with it The class money or an important document gets handed to you.
  • You do not miss a deadline The assignment is always in on time.
  • You help your classmates Explaining it again does not tire you.
  • Quiet consistency You do not stand out, and your results hold steady.
  • The dependable link in a team Your part will be ready, without fail.

Worth keeping an eye on

  • The extra load The group’s work gets finished by you at the end, every time.
  • You do not put yourself forward Even when somebody presents your work as their own, you say nothing.
  • Afraid of getting it wrong You are afraid of letting somebody down, so you do not take the risk.
  • The question stays unasked Not wanting to take up time, you leave what you did not understand alone.
  • You step back from leading Even when people trust you with it, you hand the leading to somebody else.

In relationships In friendship and close relationships

Your strengths

  • The friend they lean on On a hard day, you are the first person they call.
  • Nothing leaks Whatever is trusted to you goes no further.
  • You hold the dates You know when your friend’s exam is better than they do.
  • Honest advice You say the useful thing rather than the pleasant one, but you say it gently.
  • Long loyalty You are never the one who ends a friendship first.

Worth keeping an eye on

  • Your kindness gets used Someone who knows how kind you are makes use of it, and you act as though you had not noticed.
  • Ready at any hour You will come and help whenever you are asked, even when it costs you.
  • The hurt stays inside You collect the slights within you, and then pull away all at once.
  • You do not mention your own trouble Your friends think everything is always fine with you.
  • You agree to what does not suit you Even an invitation that is wrong for you gets a yes.
