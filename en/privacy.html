Privacy

A short, plainly written account of how Naseeb Mind handles your data.

Last updated: September 10, 2026

What is stored

When you create an account, your email and sign-in credentials are stored through Supabase Auth. The responses, scores, completion time, and unfinished progress for each challenge are linked to your account.

Optional profile details

After registration, your first name, last name, country, region, district, school, and grade are required before you can start a challenge. They are stored only for aggregate statistics and do not affect your result; you may postpone the form, but the challenge cannot start until it is complete.

Age and the figure you choose

Before the result is shown we ask your age and which historical figure you would like to see with it: the male one or the female one. Those two values are stored alongside the anonymous result and used only for aggregate analysis, such as understanding which ages use the test most. Neither a date of birth nor a birth year is requested. No name is needed to take the test. The figure you pick changes only the historical illustration and its description: your scores and your character stay exactly the same.

What is never asked

We do not ask for a phone number, exact address, date of birth, or birth year. An email and password are required for the account. Name and school details are required before a challenge starts, solely for statistics. Before the result, we ask for age and which historical figure you want to see.

Your responses

Challenge responses and scores are linked to your account. They are not anonymous: the system knows they belong to a user account with an email address.

Each completed attempt is kept separately so a later result can be compared with an earlier one. Progress on an unfinished challenge is updated so you can continue it.

A separate anonymous research copy is also sent to a private Google spreadsheet to improve the test. It contains no email or account identifier, so it cannot be joined to the record in your account. The data is not sold.

Account and email

To begin the six challenges, you create an account with an email and password. Supabase Auth handles the sign-in credentials; the password is not sent to the Google spreadsheet or another site database.

The email is used to sign in, keep responses across devices, and compare new results with earlier ones. If you ask for the guide by email, that request is stored separately.

You can ask us to delete it at any time by writing to the address below. If you are under 18, talk to a parent or teacher before leaving an email address.

On your device

Unfinished work is also kept temporarily in this device’s browser storage so a dropped connection does not lose it. The local draft is removed after the combined result appears.

Other sites

Naseeb Mind loads no advertising, tracking scripts, or external fonts. The account and its linked responses are sent to Supabase, while a separate anonymous research copy is sent to a private Google spreadsheet.

Cookies and browser storage

The site does not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels. An unfinished test draft and earlier results on this device may be kept in your browser’s localStorage; you can remove them in your browser settings.

Service providers

Accounts and account-linked responses are sent to Supabase infrastructure, while anonymous statistics are sent to a private Google Sheet. These services process data on our instructions. We do not provide data to advertising networks.

How long we keep data

We keep your account and responses while you may want to continue the test or until you ask us to delete them. When we receive a deletion request, we remove the account records and linked responses; the anonymous statistics cannot be matched to your account.

Your choices

You can ask what data we hold, request a correction, or ask us to delete your account. Write from your account email to the address below. We may check that a deletion request comes from the account owner.

If this page changes

If the way we handle data changes, we will update the wording and date on this page. Important changes will be shown on the site itself.

About the result

The result is advisory and is not a medical or scientific conclusion. Do not treat it as the sole basis for choosing a career or a field of study.

Age

The test is designed for school students aged 13-18. Those under 18 are advised to take it in consultation with a parent or teacher.

Questions

For questions, or to have your data deleted, write to: support@naseebedu.com.
