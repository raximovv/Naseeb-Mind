Accessibility

What we are doing to make Naseeb Mind usable by as many people as possible.

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Our aim

We design pages for keyboard use, phones, and different screen sizes. The language is plain, buttons are clearly labelled, and navigation stays consistent.

What we support

Headings follow a logical order, links have descriptive names, colour contrast is checked, and animations turn off when a device asks for reduced motion. Test choices can also be made with a keyboard.

Need help?

If part of a page is difficult with a keyboard, screen reader, or other assistive technology, tell us which page and device showed the problem.

Known limits

The historical figures are illustrations, and some complex images may not communicate their full meaning without visual perception. We are continuing to improve the site.

Contact

Send accessibility feedback or a problem report to: support@naseebedu.com.
