Leaders

Leader

Even when things heat up you do not raise your voice, which is why people listen to you.

When a group loses its way, you are usually the one who points out the direction.

Your strength

Making the right call under pressure.

Worth watching

Your calm can read as indifference. Do not miss the moment when the people close to you want more feeling from you.

How this character is derived

This character comes from your two strongest traits: Emotional stability and Extraversion. You have the other three as well. These two simply scored higher.

If those two traits score very closely together, the test shows you the second character too. The result is a snapshot of today, not a fixed label.

At home At home and with the people close to you

Your strengths

  • Deciding under pressure When something unexpected happens at home everyone looks at you, because you do not panic.
  • Receiving guests When people come round you start the conversation and nobody is left out.
  • Stopping an argument You say one thing without raising your voice and the row settles. Your family knows this.
  • An example to the younger ones Your younger siblings copy you even when you say nothing.
  • A shoulder to rely on You get called into the important family conversation, even though you are younger.

Worth keeping an eye on

  • Looking as if you do not care Your calm leaves the impression that it makes no difference to you. The people close to you want more feeling.
  • Silent about your own worries You hear out everyone else's problem and tell nobody yours.
  • Taking over unasked You take a situation in hand quickly, and sometimes your family only wanted you to listen.
  • The “you are strong” label People stop asking how you are, because you always look fine.
  • Avoiding the emotional talk Where there are tears you are uncomfortable, and you turn the subject to something practical.

At school In lessons, in exams and among classmates

Your strengths

  • Speaking in front of the class Your voice does not shake in a presentation. For most people that is the hardest thing.
  • Leading the group When the team has lost its way, you are the one who points somewhere.
  • Steady in an exam You do not lose what you knew to nerves, and that is worth no less than the knowledge.
  • Settling a dispute If classmates fall out, the teacher calls you.
  • Teachers rely on you The responsible job often comes to you.

Worth keeping an eye on

  • Absorbing the group's work “It will be faster”, so you do all of it. The team learns nothing.
  • Not asking for help Admitting you did not understand feels like losing the lead.
  • Drowning out the quiet ones When you speak, the shy classmate does not speak at all.
  • Bored in a slow lesson When a topic is repeated your attention fades and you miss the easy part.
  • Fear of getting it wrong The thought that everyone is watching stops you trying something new.

In relationships In friendship and close relationships

Your strengths

  • You get everyone together Whether it happens at all usually depends on you.
  • Advice without panic When a friend is lost you tell them what to actually do.
  • Easy with new people Walking into a group you do not know is not hard for you.
  • A fair judge You hear both sides and only then speak.
  • You keep your word If you said you would come, you come. Your friends count on it.

Worth keeping an eye on

  • Never showing the hard part Your friends cannot picture you struggling with anything.
  • Everyone at the same distance You are on good terms with many people and close to very few.
  • Asking is difficult You are always the one who gives, and you do not know how to take.
  • Taking charge Even meeting friends runs to your plan, and sometimes other people want to choose.
  • Cutting the feelings short A friend tells you what is wrong and you offer a solution straight away, when they wanted to be heard.

The direction that suits you best

  • ManagementMain direction

    Deciding under pressure and getting people to follow is what the job is, day to day. Planning is the side you would have to learn separately.

Alongside it, you can pick one of these three directions

  • Air traffic controlExtra

    Guiding dozens of planes at once takes exactly the calm under pressure you have.

  • Crisis negotiationExtra

    A heated room needs someone who speaks first and brings the temperature down.

  • Expedition leading and mountain guidingExtra

    In the mountains decisions are fast and the group follows you: leadership at its most real.
