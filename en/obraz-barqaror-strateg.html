How this character is derived

This character comes from your two strongest traits: Emotional stability and Conscientiousness. You have the other three as well. These two simply scored higher.

If those two traits score very closely together, the test shows you the second character too. The result is a snapshot of today, not a fixed label.

At home At home and with the people close to you

Your strengths

  • A promise is a finished job Your family does not remind you, because there is no need to.
  • Calm in a crisis When things get bad at home, you do not panic.
  • You plan far out You are the one already talking today about next year.
  • Careful with money You have spent money thoughtfully since you were small.
  • The younger ones lean on you When your younger siblings are stuck on their homework, they come to you.

Worth keeping an eye on

  • When the plan breaks An unexpected change unsettles you more than it does other people, even when none of it shows.
  • Doing instead of saying You show love and worry by doing things rather than saying them, and your family does not always read it that way.
  • Rest keeps getting postponed “Work first”, and the rest never arrives.
  • Firmness Changing a decision you have already made is hard for you.
  • You carry it alone Even when it is heavy, you pick it up yourself.

At school In lessons, in exams and among classmates

Your strengths

  • You last the distance Three months of preparation does not frighten you.
  • Steady in the exam Nerves do not wipe out what you know.
  • You study to a schedule A little every day, and by the end of the year it shows.
  • A teammate who can be relied on The part given to you will be finished, without fail.
  • An orderly notebook You can find what you wrote whenever you need it.

Worth keeping an eye on

  • Change is hard to take When the teacher changes the method, it gets difficult for you.
  • You avoid the new way You prefer the method that has been tested and do not try the other one.
  • You solve it alone What you did not understand, you try to work out on your own instead of asking.
  • The pressure to be perfect A four knocks you down harder than it knocks anyone else.
  • The open assignment “Do whatever you like” leaves you at a loss.

In relationships In friendship and close relationships

Your strengths

  • You are where you said you would be You arrive at the time you named, every time.
  • What you are told stays with you Nothing that was trusted to you travels further.
  • There on the hard day You are the first to reach a friend on their hard day.
  • Friendships that last Your friend from childhood is still your friend.
  • You speak clearly When your friend is flustered, you are the one making sense.

Worth keeping an eye on

  • Uneasy without a plan “Shall we head out right now?” throws you off.
  • Slow to let people in You are in no hurry to make new friends.
  • Nothing about yourself Your friends do not know that you struggle too.
  • You look cold Because you show so little, people take you for indifferent.
  • Forgiving is hard Someone who broke their word stays in your memory a long time.
