Frequently asked questions

Short answers.

How long does the test take?

There are six challenges. You can take them in any order and across several sittings; your account keeps both completed and unfinished work.

Will anyone see my result?

Your result is not public or visible to other students. Responses are stored in a protected account linked to your email so they can be continued and compared. Details: Privacy.

Is this test scientific?

The items are based on the Big Five, the most widely used and most extensively validated model of personality in psychology. But Naseeb Mind is not a medical or psychological diagnosis and has not yet been separately validated in an Uzbek adolescent sample. Treat the result as advisory.

What age is it for?

The test is designed for school students aged 13-18. If you are under 18, it is worth discussing the result with a parent or teacher.

How accurate is the result?

That depends entirely on how you answer. Answering as "how I am now" rather than "how I would like to be" produces a much more accurate result. Answering every item the same way still produces a result, but not one that reflects you, so consider each item separately and answer honestly.

Can my character change later?

Yes. Between 13 and 18 personality is still forming. The result is a snapshot of where you are today, not a verdict.

Why ten characters?

The two strongest of the five traits are taken. There are ten ways to choose two from five, which is where the ten characters come from. See them all.

Can I take the test again?

Yes, as often as you like. But answering thoughtfully and honestly keeps the result from jumping around.

What languages is the test available in?

Both the site and the test are available in Uzbek, Russian and English. The Russian and English items are translated from the Uzbek: the translations have not been separately validated, so results in different languages are not strictly comparable.

Ready?

Six challenges. You do not have to finish them in one day.
